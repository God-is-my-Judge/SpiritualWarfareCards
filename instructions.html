How to play

Standard play · 3–10 players

One turn, six steps

One physical deck supports up to 10 players. Agree on a winning score before you begin; five points makes a good first game.

1. Deal

Everyone keeps four Scripture cards in hand throughout the game.

2. Draw

The active player draws a Scenario card, picks Day or Night, and reads it aloud.

3. Respond

Everyone else picks the Scripture in hand that fits — using only the same side, Day or Night, as the scenario.

4. Listen

Players read key phrases aloud, or explain why their card fits.

5. Score

The active player chooses the most fitting response. That player earns one point, then refills their hand.

6. Rotate

Play passes to the left, and the new active player continues.

Day and Night

“As long as it is day, we must do the works of him who sent me. Night is coming, when no one can work.” — John 9:4

Day and Night are separate sets of 12 Scenario cards and 42 Scripture cards. Each turn, the active player chooses which set to draw from — everyone else must then respond using cards from that same side. Night carries more mature scenarios that may not suit younger players. On the website, use the Day/Night control above the table to switch.

Read the card at your pace

The skill is not to read the most words, but to recognize and speak the words that matter most. Read the summary or pull out the key words and lines—concise, Scripture-packed, and fitted to the scenario. This practices distilling a passage faithfully so its truth can be recalled and used in life. For a deeper game, read the whole card or the sections around those key phrases.

Alternate ways to play

Collaborative mode

Work as one team and agree on the strongest Scripture response. If no agreement is reached, the turn is a stalemate. Set a shared goal, such as completing ten scenarios with no more than three stalemates.

Two-player deck building

Deal four Scripture cards to each player and place four scenarios face-down. Flip the scenarios together, then play Scriptures on as many as four at once. If both players agree on the best response for a scenario, its player keeps the cards played there. The player holding the most cards when the draw pile runs out wins.

Single-player turns

Draw one Scenario card and give yourself 40 seconds to find and read the Scripture that matches it. Keep going one scenario at a time until your hand of four is spent, then reshuffle and try to beat your best score. Take turns with a friend and compare results for some friendly competition.

Personal meditation

Deal yourself four Scripture cards when your day begins. Return to them through the day and find the phrase that applies—when you’re frustrated, tempted, or blessed.
