Where your prayers live

Nothing in your prayer file is uploaded. A local browser draft saves edits automatically. Open .md offers file autosave where supported; allow the browser’s write permission. A new prayer file can use Link a file for autosave. When the status says “Autosaved to” your filename, changes are written to that file automatically. If it says “Autosaved on this device,” Export .md keeps your disk copy current.

Clearing site data can erase the browser draft and remembered file permission. Your exported or linked Markdown file remains yours.

A simple way to pray

Choose a person or situation, write concrete requests, and anchor your prayers in Scripture. Add dated observations as you notice God’s work—even when the situation remains unresolved. These original templates are inspired by Paul Miller’s prayer-card practice; the eight categories are our organization, not his official taxonomy.

Open, then edit. A first tap enlarges a card; tapping its contents opens the editor. Changes save as you type. Use **bold**, *italics*, and <u>underline</u>, or the formatting buttons. Markdown links and raw HTML other than underline are displayed as text.

Keep your file. Browsers with file-access support can autosave to a chosen .md file. Open .md requests write permission where supported; allow it to enable file autosave. For a new file, use Link a file for autosave. Other browsers save a local draft; use Export .md to update your readable copy. When you return, reconnect a remembered file if permission is needed. A recovered draft is kept until you explicitly open or reconnect a file.

People on your heart. These cards help you remember the people and needs you bring to your Father in Jesus’ name. Tell him what is on your heart, trust him in the waiting, and watch for his work in your life and theirs. We come to the Father through the Son, in the Spirit. The conversation continues beyond the cards.

Gentle reminders. Opening a card records today’s date in its Markdown metadata. Each card shows how many days it was opened during the past 30 days; opening alone does not tell the app whether you prayed. Levels count a card once per local day, and editing alone does not count. The daily rotation brings back all daily cards, 30% of weekly cards rounded up, and one as-needed card if available. Each weekly card comes around at least twice in seven days while the deck stays unchanged. You can revisit any card at any time. Simple Bible shows plain counts; quieter atmospheres tuck optional journey markers below the cards. “Your prayers are full today” is an invitation to continue with your Father, not a measure of your prayer or faith.

Offline. The downloadable HTML includes all artwork and code. A hosted copy caches its application files for offline visits after a successful first load. Browser support and storage policies vary; the download is the dependable offline copy.

Markdown structure. Each card starts with ## Title. A card comment records type, tint, art, cadence, and an optional theme override. Requests follow, with ### Scripture and ### Story below them. Avoid level-two headings inside a card; those start another card. Unknown metadata and sections are retained.

Scripture. Starter fragments are CSB excerpts, clearly marked. Add your own passages directly; the app never fetches Bible text. Scripture is God’s Word, not a promise that every requested outcome will occur. Ask with confidence in Christ and submission to the Father’s wisdom.
