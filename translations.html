Spotted a wording problem in any translation? Tell us on the workshop page — every edition here is improved by readers who know the language.

* Scenario cards awaiting a native reader. Every edition here is playable, and all of it is a work in progress. Hindi and Urdu have all of their scenario text translated but unchecked; the other Indian editions have the situation titles translated, with the three struggles beneath each one still in English. German is the only set a reader of the language has been through. The four Philippine editions, the two Brazilian ones and Swahili have no scenario cards of their own yet, so they play with the English ones. Tell us what reads wrong — naming the edition by its code (ML-IRV, TA-IRV…) tells us exactly which deck you mean.
