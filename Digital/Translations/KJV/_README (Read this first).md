# Spiritual Warfare Card Game (SWCG) — KJV Edition

A Scripture-based tool for applying God’s Word to real-life situations. It can be used for personal spiritual reflection or as a fun family or group game.

The main purpose is not simply to earn points, but to encourage and build one another up through Scripture.

*Last updated: 2026-07-19*

---

## Quick Start

1. Download all the files and save them in an easy-to-find folder.
2. Open `DeckShuffle.html` in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari. On an iPhone or iPad, see **Using Mobile Devices** below.
3. Settings should open automatically when no PDF has been selected. If they do not, click the **Settings ⚙️** icon in the upper-right corner.
4. Under **PDF File**, select one of the downloaded Scripture decks:
   * `SWCG Scripture Day KJV.pdf`
   * `SWCG Scripture Night KJV.pdf`
5. Enable **Shuffle**, then click **OK**. By default, four Scripture cards will be displayed.

### Optional Scenario Cards

If you also have a Scenario-card PDF, open **Settings ⚙️**, choose **+ Add Group**, select the Scenario PDF, and set that group to display one card.

Use Day mode for everyday and family-friendly situations. Night mode addresses more intense or mature situations and may not be appropriate for younger players.

## How to Use the Cards

### Personal Reflection

Display four Scripture cards and think about a real situation, decision, concern, temptation, blessing, or reason for gratitude.

Read the cards and choose the passage that most clearly applies. Consider:

* What truth should I remember?
* Is there a promise to trust?
* Is there an attitude or action I should change?
* How can I apply this passage today?

You may also begin the day with four cards and return to them when you feel frustrated, worried, tempted, encouraged, or blessed.

### Family or Group Play

1. Sit in a circle and choose the first active player.
2. The active player reads a Scenario card or describes a hypothetical or real situation. No one is required to share private information.
3. The other players each choose a Scripture card that could guide, strengthen, correct, comfort, or encourage the active player.
4. Each player reads the most relevant part of the passage and briefly explains the choice.
5. The active player selects the Scripture that seems most helpful or fitting.
6. Optionally, the player who presented that card receives one point.
7. Refill each hand to four Scripture cards.
8. The role of active player passes to the left.

You may play until someone reaches five points, or leave out the scoring and discuss the passages cooperatively.

The value of the game is in receiving a breadth of biblical passages and perspectives for each situation, not merely in winning.

## About DeckShuffle

DeckShuffle is a general-purpose tool that treats each page of a PDF as a card. It can also be used with other PDFs containing questions, activities, illustrations, study prompts, or similar material.

In **Settings ⚙️**, you can:

* Select one or more PDF files.
* Choose which pages to use, such as `1:42`.
* Set the number of cards to display.
* Enable **Shuffle** to randomize the cards.
* Add separate groups, such as one group for Scenarios and another for Scriptures.
* Enable **Remember settings and files between sessions**.

Click a card to view it full screen. Use **Replace** to remove it and display the next card.

The selected files remain in your browser and are not uploaded to a server.

## Using Mobile Devices

### iPhone and iPad

`DeckShuffle.html` probably will not work correctly when opened directly in the Files app preview. The preview may display the page without allowing all of its JavaScript features to run.

For more reliable results, use a local web-server app such as **WorldWideWeb**:

1. Place the HTML file and PDFs in the same folder.
2. Select that folder in the local-server app.
3. Start the server.
4. Open the local address provided by the app in Safari.

This serves the files as a small local website rather than opening the HTML file in preview mode.

### Android

On Android, open the HTML file in a full browser rather than in the file manager’s preview mode. On many devices, this will be sufficient.

Behavior varies between browsers and file managers. If the page does not work correctly, use a local web-server app and open the local address it provides in your browser.

## How to play

### 1\. Open DeckShuffle

Open `DeckShuffle.html` in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari). No installation or internet connection is required after the first load — PDF.js is cached by your browser automatically.

### 2\. Open Settings

Click the **⚙️ gear icon** in the top-right corner of the app.

### 3\. Load your PDFs

In the Settings panel:

1. Click **+ Add Group** to add a card deck.
2. Under **PDF file**, click the file picker and navigate to one of the PDF files above (e.g., `SWCG Scripture Day KJV.pdf`).
3. In the **Pages** field, enter the page range you want to use — for example `1:40` to use pages 1 through 40, or `1,3,5` to pick specific pages. The app will show a preview of how many pages were parsed.
4. Set **Cards to display** to however many cards you want face-up at once.
5. Check **Randomize** to shuffle the deck (recommended), or leave it unchecked for sequential order.
6. Repeat steps 1–5 to add more groups (e.g., a Scenario group and a Scripture group).

### 4\. Save & Play

Click **Save & Play**. Your cards will appear on the table. Click any card to view it full-screen. Click **Replace** on a card to draw the next one from the shuffled deck.

### 5\. Tips

* Check **Remember settings & files between sessions** to save your configuration — the PDF stays in your browser (IndexedDB) and is not uploaded anywhere.
* Day and Night modes each have their own Scenario and Scripture PDFs — mix and match as your group prefers.
* The **Advanced** section (in Settings) can hide an encrypted copy of any PDF's text inside a PNG image using AES-256-GCM steganography. This is an optional feature for archiving.
