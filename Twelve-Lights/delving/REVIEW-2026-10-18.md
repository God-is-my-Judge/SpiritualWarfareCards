# The Delving: October 18 review

The forty-door descent already has a strong identity: painted stone-elven halls, varied number puzzles, twelve breastpiece stones, optional chambers and a dawn ending. The best improvements preserve that journey while making its controls and progress more dependable.

Before editing, the current game, artwork and source art pack were committed and pushed to GitLab as `c35232f` on `india-german-editions`. Unrelated working-tree changes were excluded.

## Findings and changes

| Finding | Improvement |
| --- | --- |
| A ledger visit or numeral toggle redrew the puzzle and discarded unfinished answers; reloads also lost solved intermediate steps. | Save drafts and step progress under the existing storage key, with compatibility for earlier saves. |
| Match destinations were divs and formula destinations were spans. Keyboard users could select tokens but could not activate destinations. | Use native buttons for destinations, announce placement, preserve focus between steps, and submit number answers with Enter. |
| The ledger allowed keyboard focus to leave the overlay. | Keep focus in the dialog, make the game underneath inert, and restore focus on close. |
| Numeric checking removed arbitrary characters, accepting `4/0` and `4e0` as `40`. | Parse a single complete number with optional units or an explicitly accepted word answer. Preserve alternate values and tolerances. |
| Two prompts said “choose all” but accepted two of three correct choices. | Require all three there; explicitly allow at least two in the eighth-day puzzle. |
| Shuffle mode grouped the ledger in canonical act order and could lead to dawn while earlier doors remained shut after changing modes. | Show the actual shuffled stair and route players back to unopened doors before the ending. |
| Progress was difficult to gauge outside the ledger. | Add forty carved marks with current-door and opened-door states, plus textual door and stone totals. |
| The mine had atmosphere but little companionship or initial guidance. | Add field notes and the Keeper of the Lamps, with ten original act-opening lines about reading, patient work and humility. |
| Narrow layouts squeezed matching grids and lamp controls. | Let match columns fit the available width, preserve space for arrows and enlarge phone tap targets. |
| Tiny pointer movements started drags, drops could cross puzzle boundaries, and successful drags left cancellation listeners behind. | Add a movement threshold, scope drops to their puzzle, highlight destinations and clean up every completion/cancellation. |

The existing distinction between explicit biblical statements, inference, interpretation and manuscript variants remains central. This pass corrects prompt/checker inconsistencies and adds fictional guide dialogue; it does not perform a new historical or theological source audit. The paintings remain decorative rather than answer data.

## Verification

- Full browser playthrough: 42 chambers, all seven interaction types, 40 main doors, 12 stones and both keepsakes; progress survives reload.
- Existing feature checks: alternate numeric answers, Elvish numerals, shuffle and per-chamber lighting.
- Artwork checks: 77 assets available, door placement within 0.01 px, lamp waking, breastpiece reveal and fallback without the art manifest.
- Full view: matching and the next puzzle step remain playable; door opening shows lore; Escape exits.
- Regression assertions: saved arrangements and typed answers, intermediate steps, keyboard placement, incomplete answers, malformed numbers, ledger focus, shuffled ordering, ending navigation, reset and storage failures.
- Layout checks: order, match, formula and numeric puzzles fit widths of 320, 390 and 768 px. Desktop, phone and phone full-view screenshots were inspected in Chromium.
- Zero browser errors in the completed runs; JavaScript syntax and Git whitespace checks pass.

## Remaining opportunities

The Delving was released later on October 18: the preview gate is gone and the tab shows for everyone. Additional riddles could deepen the late-game variety once their wording and sources are reviewed. Short passage transitions and occasional gem-specific guide lines are useful future additions. Browser checks here use Chromium; Safari and Firefox have not been exercised in this pass.
