Why games?

A game is one of the oldest ways people gather. It slows a family down long enough to sit at the same table. It gives a nervous student something to do with their hands while they learn. It gives a prayer list a place to live. It turns an ordinary evening into time two people spend on purpose, together.

None of that is a distraction from the life of faith. Eating, drinking, laughing, praying, and talking late into the night can all be done for the glory of God, as gifts received with thanks rather than time merely spent. The games collected here are made in that spirit: modest, joyful, and built so that the Word of God is never far from the table.

Bring a game
